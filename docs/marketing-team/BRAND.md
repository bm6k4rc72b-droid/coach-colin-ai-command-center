## Guardrails (both businesses)
- **Health claims:** coaching is not medical advice. Never give peptide or GLP-1 dosing, injection or stacking
  instructions; never say a product cures, treats or prevents disease; never call a product FDA approved unless that
  exact product is. Blood work and a doctor come first.
- **Cosmetic vs drug claims (FDA):** cosmetics may say they *improve the look* of skin or hair ("reduces the
  appearance of fine lines"). Claims that change the body's structure or function ("regrows hair", "boosts collagen
  production", "repairs DNA", "heals acne", "SPF" without monograph testing) make it a drug. Amazon and TikTok Shop
  enforce the same line.
- **Disclosure (FTC):** any affiliate, sponsored or gifted content says so up front (`#ad`, "I earn a commission").
- **Proof:** no invented statistics, reviews, testimonials, before/after numbers or "featured on" logos.
- **Outreach:** CAN-SPAM for email (real sender, physical address, working unsubscribe, honest subject); TCPA consent
  for SMS; no scraping of platforms whose terms forbid it; use licensed data (Apollo, Sales Navigator, platform
  creator marketplaces).
- Agents draft. A human approves anything that is posted, sent or spent.
