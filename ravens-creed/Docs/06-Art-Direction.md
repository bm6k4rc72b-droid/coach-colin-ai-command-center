# Art Direction

**North star:** *saga realism, lit like a painting.* Grounded, tactile Viking-Age material culture rendered with
cinematic, moody light, gold catching in darkness, and the raven as a recurring sign. Reference: your concept images
(the rain-soaked cliff town with timber stairways, the stave hall above a fjord under aurora and lightning, Bjorn's
character sheet, the gear sheet).

## Pillars

| Pillar | Means | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark, moody, legible** | Low-key lighting with strong motivated sources (hearths, torches, lightning, low sun under storm decks). Silhouettes read at gameplay distance. | Muddy flat grey; crushed blacks that hide traversal paths |
| **Gold in the dark** | Aged gold accents (tooling, brooches, embroidery, sigils) placed where light hits: the eye goes to gold | Shiny new gold; gold everywhere |
| **Material truth** | Wet thatch, oiled wood, rusted rivets, wolf fur, waxed leather, wool. Everything shows weather and use | Clean, plastic, uniform surfaces |
| **Raven motif** | Ravens in silhouettes, carvings, cloaks, UI frames, Raven Sight, the order's sign | Literal raven icons on everything |
| **Scale and verticality** | Cliffs, stairways, towers and masts frame every settlement; every town has a high point to climb | Flat sprawling towns |

Rule of thumb: 70% desaturated darks, 25% cold storm mids, 5% warm accents (gold, fire). Blood is dark and sparing.

## Characters

- **Bjorn:** silhouette = fur mantle shoulders plus long split cloak with the gold raven on the back; shaved sides and
  braided crown; rune tattoos (brow, cheek); gold-tooled bracers (the left hides the wrist-seax). Three armour tiers
  with weather variants (wet fur clumps, frost on the mantle, snow on the shoulders).
- **Clansfolk:** wool in natural dyes (madder red, woad blue, undyed), tablet-woven trims, brooches. Jarls carry more
  gold and fur.
- **Anglo-Saxons:** linen and wool, softer colours, crosses and book-satchels for clergy, mail for thegns.
- **The Circle:** outwardly ordinary rich folk; tell-tale branded sun-wheel rings and gilt that is a little too new.
- **Tech:** MetaHuman-based heads for named characters, with custom grooms (beards, braids) and skin texture detail for
  scars and tattoos; strand-based hair and fur on hero characters and card-based on crowds; Chaos Cloth for cloaks.

## Environments

- **Fjords:** sheer wet cliffs (Nanite photogrammetry), waterfalls, birch and pine, mist decks at mid-height, timber
  stairways and walkways bolted to rock, thatch and turf roofs.
- **Valdrheim (slice hero):** the stave hall on its promontory with dragon-head gables; palisade; oath-stone; watchtower
  with an iron brazier; boathouse at the foot of the cliff stairs.
- **England:** Roman ruins under Saxon timber (Jórvík), monastery stone (Lindisfarne), green rolling Wessex under rain.
- **Wear:** every surface uses the weather master material (wetness, snow, puddles); decals for soot, mud and rot.

## Lighting and post-processing

- Lumen GI and reflections; Virtual Shadow Maps; Volumetric Cloud shadows.
- Time-of-day presets with designer overrides per mission (the Valdrheim storm is a locked look).
- Post: filmic tonemapper, gentle bloom on fire and lightning, subtle film grain, vignette in interiors, chromatic
  aberration off by default. Two LUTs blended by weather (warm hearth vs. cold storm).
- Interiors: hearth key light, smoke haze (local fog volumes), shafts through smoke holes.

## UI

Dark parchment and iron frames with aged-gold rules; serif display face for titles, readable humanist sans for body;
raven-feather point-of-no-return icon; diegetic Raven Sight colours (gold target, red threat, blue ally, white loot,
teal clue) with colour-blind alternatives.

## Original-IP guardrails

The game is in the genre of historical stealth-action RPGs but uses only its own names, symbols and terms: the Raven
Wardens and the Gullbrand Circle (not any existing franchise's orders), the raven sign, Raven Sight, Raven's Plunge and
the wrist-seax. No logos, faction names or trademarked terms from existing games appear in assets or text.
