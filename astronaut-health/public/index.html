Astronaut blood panels: Inspiration4

Routine blood tests (complete blood count and metabolic panel) from the four Inspiration4 crew members, before and after their 3-day flight in September 2021. Each person is compared only with their own preflight baseline.

Made by Colin Cook

Missing values

These values are absent or reported as “NA” in the source files. They are left empty here, never filled in.

    Data: NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR), Inspiration4 crew OSD-569 (complete blood count, LSDS-7) and OSD-575 (comprehensive metabolic panel, LSDS-8), SUBMITTED files. Crew IDs are the anonymized IDs from the source.

    eGFR is excluded (outdated race-based formula). BUN/creatinine ratio is shown but not scored (mostly missing).

    For education only, not medical interpretation.
