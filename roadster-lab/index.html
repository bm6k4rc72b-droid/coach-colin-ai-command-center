Launch trace: speed and g

Cyan = speed, magenta = acceleration. Amber shading marks where tyre grip, not the motors, is the limit. The plateau is μ·g or τ/(r·m); the fall-off is constant power, F = P/v.

Force budget: where the grip runs out

The car accelerates by the gap between what it can push (the lower of motors and grip) and what the air takes. Where the lines meet is top speed.

Range against steady speed

Green = this car, amber = the original Roadster. Drag rises with v², so range falls fast above motorway speed.

The Roadster in orbit · Kepler's laws

Solves E − e·sin E = M for each date. Two-body model: approximate, no planetary perturbations.
