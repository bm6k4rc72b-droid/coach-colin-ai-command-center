          Privacy

          Nothing leaves this device unless you turn on narration below or press Export. There is no face recognition here and no identity of any kind: a subject is a number that is reused the moment they leave.

          Narration (optional)

          Every summary is written on-device from the measurements. A language model can restate one in plainer words and answer questions about it. What is sent is the numbers — no image, no crop, no location.

          Narration off.

          What this cannot do

          • See through walls. No browser exposes a radio, and no phone exposes WiFi channel data. The Through walls tab connects a real sensor if you have one; with nothing attached it reports nothing rather than guessing.
          • Measure temperature. Ironbow is a palette over ordinary brightness. A white shirt reads hot. Thermal needs a long-wave infrared sensor.
          • Recognise anyone. No faces are matched, stored or compared, here or anywhere else.
          • Read intent. Cadence, pauses and reversals are movement. They are not motives, and the summaries never claim otherwise.
          • Take medical measurements. Pulse and breathing come from a camera, need a still, close, well-lit subject, and are usually unavailable — which is what the app will say.
