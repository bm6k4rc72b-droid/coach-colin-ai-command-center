How this page was made. The backdrop is a WebGL fragment shader — anamorphic streak flares, vertically-oval bokeh, elliptical vignette and film grain, rendered live rather than played from a file, which is why it responds to where you are on the page. The operator is drawn from a joint rig on a 2D canvas; his gait is driven by ground covered rather than by a timer, so scrolling back walks him backwards through the same footfalls. He is an original design, not a character from anything.

Where the words come from. Every feature claim on this page is read out of the console's own capability ledger at load time, wearing whatever state that ledger gives it. There is no second copy of the marketing text for anyone to sweeten later. The voices are your device's built-in speech engine, offline and free, reading those same rows.
