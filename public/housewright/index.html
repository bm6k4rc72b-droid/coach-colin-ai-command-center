Measure the property by pointing at it.

Hold the phone at a known height, aim where the wall meets the floor, and hold a fingertip still in the frame. Each dwell drops a corner. Four corners make a room; the rooms make a plan, a massing model, and a ranked list of what is worth spending money on.
