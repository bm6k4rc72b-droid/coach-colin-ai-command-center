Software stack & cited studies

UcantSeeMeX is a privacy-first visibility controller. The live demo in this page runs entirely in the browser. Field hardware (thermal cores, CSI radios, drones) is specified below so a production build can swap the simulated layers for real sensors.

1. Human segmentation + pinch control (core engine)

The invisibility effect is classic background replacement driven by a person mask. On calibrate, the current frame is stored as a background plate. When stealth is on, every person pixel in the MediaPipe mask is punched out and replaced with that plate, so you disappear from your own camera feed.

  • @mediapipe/selfie_segmentation — MobileNetV3-based person/background mask. General model 256×256; landscape model 144×256 (used here, modelSelection = 1). Designed for close-range selfie / conferencing (< 2 m).
  • @mediapipe/hands — 21-landmark hand tracker. Pinch = Euclidean distance between landmark 4 (thumb tip) and 8 (index tip) below 0.045 normalized units, debounced at 700 ms.
  • Canvas 2D compositing — destination-out with the mask, then destination-over with the stored background. No pixels leave the device.

MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation docs and model card (Google AI Edge). Hands solution: Zhang et al., “MediaPipe Hands: On-device Real-time Hand Tracking,” 2020. Open implementations of the same cloak pattern: github.com/rakeshkattimani25/ghost-invisibility, github.com/harshbhadani4597/ghost-invisibility-effect, github.com/haripriya262007-blip/invisibility-mode, github.com/khyathi-2006/invisibility-computer-vision, github.com/tubakhxn/Invisibility-Computer-Vision.

2. Thermal + night-vision layer

This browser build applies an inferno-style luminance colormap (thermal) or a contrast/teal night boost. That is a visualization, not a radiometric sensor. Production pairing:

  • FLIR Lepton 3.5 / PureThermal or Seek Compact for true LWIR.
  • RGB + thermal fusion for segmentation when cloth texture matches the wall — the exact failure mode called out in the product notes.
  • Classic thermal detection: capture a background heat plate, subtract, threshold human-shaped blobs (connected components). RGB selfie-seg fills holes when IR contrast collapses.

Background-subtraction human detection is the standard baseline in infrared surveillance literature (e.g. Davis & Sharma multimodal IR/visible tracking). Fusion of thermal + RGB for robust person masks is widely used in night-time ADAS and search-and-rescue stacks.

3. Motion sensor

In the web app, motion uses the browser DeviceMotionEvent IMU (accelerometer magnitude vs ~9.8 m/s²). Spikes increment the radar hit counter. On phone / glasses / drone firmware the same panel should read:

  • Hardware PIR + IMU fusion on wearables.
  • Optical flow on the camera pipeline as a second vote.
  • Wi-Fi CSI motion (below) as the through-wall channel.

4. Wi-Fi CSI presence (production radio, simulated here)

Browsers cannot expose raw Channel State Information. The waveform panel is an honest visualization driven by motion energy. A shipping multi-device build should use:

  • ESP32 CSI (Espressif esp-csi / ESP-IDF) or Nexmon-patched Broadcom / Raspberry Pi.
  • Intel 5300 CSI tool for lab capture.
  • Pipeline: Hampel + Savitzky–Golay denoise → segment CSI streams → CNN / ResNet or SVM / LSTM classifier.

Published accuracies this stack is designed around: device-free CSI presence + counting 98.75% presence / 97.78% 0–5 person count (IEEE; Hampel + Savitzky–Golay + CNN/ResNet); CSI presence methodology >90% average (Sensors 2023, MDPI); Amazon/UW home presence work targeting >90% true-positive on commodity CSI; activity + fall recognition with SVM and LSTM on 802.11n CSI (Damodaran et al., CCF TPCI 2020). RSSI-only upward spikes are a coarse fallback; CSI subcarriers are the real feature.

6. Multi-device ecosystem

Target clients from the product art: iPhone (AVFoundation + MediaPipe iOS or Vision person segmentation), Android (CameraX + MediaPipe Tasks), laptop dashboard (this web app / Electron), smart glasses AR HUD (WebXR or vendor OS), drone thermal gimbal (RTSP + LWIR), IoT camera (on-edge mask, MQTT events). Transport: encrypted WebRTC or MQTT over TLS. No raw video leaves the originating device unless the owner explicitly shares a processed feed.

7. What this is — and is not

Product line on the posters: “privacy creator concept · own your visibility · not surveillance evasion.” The cloak only rewrites your camera composite. It does not jam radios, blind other people’s cameras, or defeat physical observers. Use it for creator privacy, background replacement, and demos of on-device CV.
