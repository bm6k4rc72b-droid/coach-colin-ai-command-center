CITED RESEARCH THE COACH USES

  1. Wang et al., BMC Sports Sci Med Rehabil (2026). VBT vs PBT meta-analysis: jump SMD 0.27; COD SMD 0.45; strength and sprint not significantly different. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13102-025-01504-9
  2. Pareja-Blanco et al., Scand J Med Sci Sports (2017). VL20 vs VL40 squat: similar 1RM, better CMJ at 20% VL; more hypertrophy + IIX loss at 40% VL.
  3. Pareja-Blanco et al. / related VL work: low-to-moderate velocity loss (≈10–25%) favours strength-power efficiency; higher VL accumulates volume for hypertrophy.
  4. Weakley / Pérez-Castilla line of VBT reviews: VL magnitude is a primary configuration variable of the stimulus.
  5. Bazarevsky et al., BlazePose / MediaPipe Pose. 33 landmarks; Heavy model ~97% PCK@0.2 on HIIT.
  6. WiFlow (arXiv 2602.08661): Wi-Fi CSI continuous pose, PCK@20 97%, MPJPE 8 mm on their set, 4.82M params.
  7. RePos (2026): cross-environment Wi-Fi 3D pose; 10–21% MPJPE drop vs prior CSI methods on MM-Fi.
  8. Geng / Person-in-WiFi-3D (CVPR 2024) and DensePose From WiFi (arXiv 2301.00250): 17 keypoints + dense UV from CSI amplitude/phase.
  9. Jiao et al., Sensors (2021): IRT difference maps classify working muscle (biceps/triceps/deltoid) at 92.3% CNN accuracy.
  10. Bo et al., Med Biol Eng Comput (2021): IRT temperature tracks sEMG RMS/MF during fatiguing calf raises.
  11. Perpetuini et al., Sensors (2023): ML on IRT estimates EMG ARV (r = 0.886) and MDF (r = 0.661) in squats — proxy, not a replacement for EMG.

Honest limits: commodity Wi-Fi CSI APIs are not exposed on iPhone/Android for third-party apps. Production “invisible mode” needs a paired CSI access point or research NIC. Phone thermal cameras are uncommon; most users get pose + IMU only. Numbers in the HUD during Simulate Set are a teaching model, not lab-grade force plates.
