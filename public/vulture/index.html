      RTSP relay for IP cameras, DJI and drones (runs on your own machine)

      Browsers can't open rtsp:// directly. The relay is a 120-line Node script that has ffmpeg transcode RTSP to MJPEG, which the browser then reads. Video stays on your network.

      curl -O 
node rtsp-relay.mjs          # needs Node 18+ and ffmpeg
# → http://127.0.0.1:8787  (HOST=0.0.0.0 to reach it from a phone)
