HOW IT WORKS — BEST AVAILABLE STACK

This control center is a high-fidelity product prototype. Sensor numbers in the HUD are simulated unless a browser sensor is enabled. Production hardware and model classes are listed below with the actual literature — including limits.

LayerBest software / hardwareWhat the studies actually show
Face / anger / AU MediaPipe Face Landmarker → MobileNetV2 or Affdex-class AU model (AFFDEX 2.0). OpenFace 2.0 as open fallback. On-device TFLite / Core ML. Affectiva Affdex tracks with facial EMG on posed happy/angry faces (Stöckli et al.). MobileNetV2 FER-2013 ~74% 7-class. AFFDEX 2.0 reports stronger in-the-wild AU accuracy than OpenFace on large frame sets. Posed ≠ spontaneous interrogation.
Thermal night vision FLIR Lepton / Boson or Seek Thermal on glasses + drone gimbal. Periorbital + forehead ROI tracker (Pavlidis-style) + rPPG fusion. Pavlidis et al., Nature 2002: 83% mock-crime vs 70% polygraph on n=20. Tsiamyrtzis 2007: 87.2% automated periorbital. Springer EURASIP 2024: 89–93% combining forehead + periorbital in lab video. Change onset ~300 ms from sympathetic flow. Lab, not courtroom.
Invisible motion (Wi-Fi CSI) ESP32-S3 CSI nodes or Wi-Fi 6E CSI (Intel/Nexmon). Aggregator: amplitude+phase, EMD / MVDR beamform, EfficientNetV2 / Wi-SensiNet HAR. This is the “no camera” ghost sensor. IEEE 2024: Wi-SensiNet through-wall HAR high accuracy on constrained 7-class sets; Wi-locind respiration error <0.3 bpm with arrays; ICIP 2024 long-range NLOS HAR ~87–92% with directional ESP32-S3. Chest motion 1–5 mm is detectable in research setups. Environment-specific calibration required.
Voice openSMILE eGeMAPS + wav2vec 2.0 / Whisper embeddings for arousal, jitter, shimmer, pause. NOT consumer “voice lie detectors”. Classic VSA / CVSA / LVA gadgets perform at chance for deception (NRC 2003; DoD / NIJ evaluations). Voice is useful as an arousal channel in a fusion model — it is not a lie detector by itself.
Fusion / “lie score” Calibrated gradient-boosted or transformer late fusion with uncertainty. Never a single percentage of “lying”. Multimodal visual+thermal emotion nets report 90%+ in paper datasets. Deception is not an emotion. Combined thermal + polygraph lab figures near 92% do not transfer to open meetings without ground truth, countermeasures, or bias audit.
Glasses + drone + sync WebXR / manufacturer SDK (XREAL, Meta). DJI Mobile SDK + thermal payload. Transport: WebRTC + MQTT, TLS 1.3, libsodium E2E. On-device first, cloud optional. Product integration, not a detection paper. Latency target in mockups: 8–22 ms local; cloud adds more.
