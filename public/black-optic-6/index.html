Offline

Point a camera at the ground you want watched. Everything runs on this device — frames are measured and discarded, nothing is uploaded, and there is no face recognition anywhere in this console.

Calibration

Pixels

Until the view is calibrated the console reports pixels and refuses to convert them. Type the camera's mounting and it reports metres — height, ground speed, distance — and the horizon line appears so you can check the answer.

Camera picker

Scan

A DJI Pocket or Action in USB webcam mode is a standard UVC device — it turns up here like any other camera and the whole detection chain runs on it, gimbal and all. So does a GoPro in webcam mode, and any HDMI capture card.

                        Sonar map

                        No sonar

                        Beam returns build an occupancy map — free water in front of the return, bottom or obstacle at it, the beam drawn as the cone it actually is. Underwater there is no satellite fix, so the position drifts; each scan is matched against the map already built to pull it back, and the drift estimate is shown rather than hidden.

                        No sonar is attached. A rehearsal generates synthetic returns from a made-up dam so the mapping can be seen working — every number it produces is synthetic and badged as modelled.
