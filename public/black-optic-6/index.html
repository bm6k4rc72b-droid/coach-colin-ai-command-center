Optics

Offline

Point a camera at the ground you want watched. Everything runs on this device — frames are measured and discarded, nothing is uploaded, and there is no face recognition anywhere in this console.

Calibration

Pixels

Until the view is calibrated the console reports pixels and refuses to convert them. Type the camera's mounting and it reports metres — height, ground speed, distance — and the horizon line appears so you can check the answer.

Camera sources

Linked

Any camera that publishes a stream this browser can open — HLS, WebRTC, or an MJPEG URL — becomes another view. Vendor-cloud-only cameras cannot be used, because the stream never reaches this device.
